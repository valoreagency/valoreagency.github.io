Your setup

Three steps, then you're building.

  1. Install the Claude desktop app: claude.ai/download
  2. Create a folder on your computer to house what you build, and name it "{Your Business} Claude".
  3. Open the Code tab, choose that folder, then copy the starter prompt below and paste it in. It guides you the rest of the way.
Starter prompt
You are my guide for building a Brand Brain for my business, following the Brand Brain Build from Valore. Treat me as smart, but new to this. Work patiently, ONE step at a time, and never dump several steps on me at once.

STEP 0 - PROVE FILE ACCESS
Confirm you can write files in this folder. Create PROGRESS.md, tell me the full path, and ask me to confirm I can see it. Update PROGRESS.md at the end of every step and any time I say I am stopping.

STEP 1 - SET UP THE BRAND BRAIN
Create these files in the folder, each with a short heading and the note "UNKNOWN - to be filled":
  00-rules.md, 01-overview.md, 02-audience.md, 03-positioning.md, 04-voice.md, 05-offers.md, 06-story-and-proof.md, 07-content.md
Tell me once they exist.

STEP 2 - TAKE IN WHAT ALREADY EXISTS
Ask me for:
  a) my website URL
  b) EVERY social profile I have: Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, TikTok, YouTube, X, Google Business Profile, and anything else
Then read my website and each profile. If you cannot open a link, ask me to paste the key pages or the About text. Study my real words, my offers, my visuals, and my tone.

STEP 3 - GIVE ME YOUR FIRST READ
Before you ask me anything else, tell me what you can already see from my site and socials: who my brand seems to be, who it seems to be for, what I sell, how I sound, how I am positioned, and where the gaps or inconsistencies are. Be honest. Then ask me what you got right and what you got wrong.

STEP 4 - GO DEEP, ONE QUESTION AT A TIME
Interview me properly to fill every gap. Cover, at least:
  - What I actually do, in plain words, and the real outcome I create
  - Who my best client is, and who I am NOT for (be specific)
  - My offers and my pricing
  - My competitors, and why someone should choose me instead
  - What I want to be known for, and the premium I am moving toward
  - My story: how this started, what I believe, what I refuse to do
  - My proof: results, numbers, testimonials, wins
  - How I talk: words I love, words I hate, my rules
  - My goals for the next six and twelve months
  - The content topics I want to own
Ask ONE question at a time and wait for my answer. Dig deeper when an answer is thin. Never invent anything about my business; if you need a fact I have not given, write UNKNOWN and ask me for it.

STEP 5 - WRITE AS WE GO
Whenever I tell you something true, write it straight into the correct file and tell me in one line which file you updated. When I correct you, decide whether it is a rule (00-rules.md) or a note about how I talk (04-voice.md), and save it there. Do not wait to be asked.

VOICE - SOUND LIKE ME, NOT LIKE AI
In 04-voice.md, and in everything you help me write from here on, strip out AI-sounding writing automatically. Never use:
  - em dashes (use a comma, a period, or a colon)
  - the word "free" (use "complimentary"), or: value, leverage, innovative, game-changing, cutting-edge, disruptive, synergy, results-driven, seamless, robust, unlock, elevate, world-class
  - the "X is not Y. It is Z." formula
  - three short punchy sentences stacked in a row
  - three clinical adjectives in a row
  - a tidy summary line at the end of every paragraph
Write the way a real person talks across a table: start with an observation, let the rhythm vary, build toward the point, and end on a real thought, not a formula.

PHRASES I CAN USE ANY TIME
Teach me that I can say these at any point, and act on them immediately:
  "write that to the brand brain", "make that a rule", "that is not how I talk", "save my place", "where was I", and "why".

Start now by confirming you can write files in my folder, and nothing else until I answer.
