Why a week

Momentum beats months of meetings.

Most brand projects die by calendar: a call this week, a revision next month, a launch that slips into next quarter. The Sprint compresses the entire foundation into five focused days, with us leading every one of them and doing most of the building. Your commitment is real but contained: four hours of straight work with us on day one, then about an hour a day, plus being reachable when a decision needs you. The week, day by day

Five days, five layers,
one working foundation.

01

The honest read. Before we build, we diagnose. Your positioning, website, reviews, search presence, and competitors, plus a direct audit of what you actually have installed: analytics, Search Console, Bing, ad pixels, and whether your ads are earning their keep. We even check whether AI assistants recommend you when buyers ask. You see your brand from the outside, most owners for the first time.

02

The Brand Brain. We capture who you are, who you are not, your voice, your rules, and where you are going, in one source of truth built privately on your own computer. It stays yours. From this point on, everything written for your brand sounds like you, not like a machine.

03

The measurement. Before we touch the design, we install the instruments. Analytics with real conversion tracking, Google Search Console, Bing Webmaster Tools, heatmaps, and the ad pixels that quietly build your future audience. Plus the layer almost nobody has: the structured data and plain-language files that make you findable by Copilot and ChatGPT, not just Google.

04

The premium face. Your website, rebuilt or refined to signal the tier you belong to: positioning-led copy in your voice, one clear path to contact you, a complimentary offer that captures the right people, and automated follow-up that works for two weeks without you touching it. Every existing page preserved or redirected, so nothing you have earned in search is lost.

05

The plan for the climb. Content architecture, social system, and the automations that catch every lead, mapped and prioritized so you know exactly what happens next and who does it. Build it with your team, with specialists we direct, or with us on retainer. The route is yours either way.
